I spent a long time building things and not telling anyone about them. This site is the correction.

The plan is straightforward: ship things, write down what I learned, and let the record accumulate. Not a launch announcement every time — just a running log of what I am working on and what turned out to be harder than expected.

## What I'm working on now

**Stories Restored** is live. It restores old family photographs using AI, with a free path for one photo and a paid path for an album. It is the most finished thing I have built and the first project on this site.

Several other projects are partly built and will land here as they get finished.

## Why this matters for the work I take on

Most of what I do is the same shape regardless of the client: take a problem that sounds like it needs a large system, find the version that can ship this month, and put it in front of real users before the architecture gets interesting.

Writing that down publicly keeps me honest about it.
